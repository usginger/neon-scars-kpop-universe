# ⚡ SCENE 04: THE MAIN FRAME CLIMAX

**INT. THE APEX BROADCAST RECON - MAINFRAME TOWER - NIGHT**

A massive, cathedral-like chamber surrounded by floor-to-ceiling glass windows overlooking a storm-ravaged Neo-Seoul. Billions of streaming data bits pulse down vertical fiber-optic pillars, bathing the white polished-marble floors in a frantic, algorithmic glow.

In the center of the room stands the **CORPORATE MASTERMIND** (40s, immaculate three-piece suit accented with sharp red cybernetic piping). He stands completely motionless, hands folded behind his back, calmly watching a floating multi-panel holographic projection of the city grid.

The heavy glass security doors behind him suddenly shatter inward. A shockwave of pure sonic vibration tears through the air. 

KEANU steps through the smoke, a smoking EMP pulse-rifle leveled in his hands. His wrapped ribcage is visibly strained, dark blood beginning to seep back through the clean white bandages. 

Beside him, the **FEMALE LEAD** strides forward. Her posture is lethal, unyielding. Her blonde hair is windswept, and her brilliant neon-blue eyes are blazing at maximum luminescent output—the internal lenses whirring and auto-focusing with mechanical precision.

The Corporate Mastermind doesn't even flinch. He slowly turns around, a cold, clinical smile pulling at his lips.

**CORPORATE MASTERMIND**
Impressive. You survived the highway interceptors, traversed the service shafts, and bypassed a level-five automated firewall. All to bring my property right back to my office desk.

**KEANU**
(Leveling his pulse-rifle directly at the Mastermind's chest)
She isn't your property. And this isn't a return. It's a system wide eviction notice.

**CORPORATE MASTERMIND**
A romantic sentiment, Keanu. But let us look at the empirical metrics. 

With a slick flick of his wrist, the Mastermind pulls up a flashing red diagnostic screen tracking her vital telemetry.

```text
[WARNING: CHRONIC NEURAL CORRUPTION DETECTED]
[CYBERNETIC OPTICAL TEMPERATURE: 99.4%]
[CRITICAL FAILSAFE OVERRIDE IN 60 SECONDS]
```

**CORPORATE MASTERMIND**
(Looking directly at the Female Lead)
Your visual cortex is experiencing total thermal collapse. Every second you stay disconnected from our server nodes, your core matrix is eroding into binary dust. You aren't liberating yourself. You are deleting your own consciousness.

The Female Lead steps ahead of Keanu, pushing past the barrel of his rifle. She stops directly in front of the central transmitter console—the main broadcast pillar that controls the propaganda array for the entire continent.

**FEMALE LEAD**
(Voice vibrating with a multi-layered digital harmonic)
Then I'll make sure my final upload is something this city will never forget.

She slams her bandaged palms directly into the terminal port interfaces. 

Intense blue data tendrils violently arc out from the console, wrapping around her forearms. She gasps, her spine arching backward as the sheer velocity of the mainframe's throughput flows directly through her nervous system. 

```text
[MASTER CIPHER KEY DETECTED]
[GLOBAL BROADCAST PROTOCOL ACTIVATED...]
[DECRYPTING ALL REBEL TRANSMISSIONS...]
```

**CORPORATE MASTERMIND**
(His clinical composure finally shattering)
Disconnect her! Immediately!

The Mastermind reaches into his suit lapel, pulling a sleek, high-frequency plasma blade. He springs forward with lethal corporate speed, aiming directly for the optical link cables running up her neck.

Keanu moves instantly, intercepting him mid-stride. He rams the butt of his pulse-rifle into the Mastermind’s jaw, forcing him back. The plasma blade slashes through the air, shearing Keanu's shoulder. Keanu grunts through the blinding pain, dropping his spent rifle and tackling the Mastermind hard onto the polished marble floor.

They engage in a brutal, close-quarters struggle beneath the pulsing terminal. Keanu catches the Mastermind’s wrist just inches from his throat, veins bulging against his leather cuffs.

High above them on the central pedestal, the Female Lead's eyes go blindingly white. The neon blue expands until it consumes her entire visual field.

**FEMALE LEAD**
(Screaming over the roar of the network data stream)
Keanu! The bridge is open! Initiate the final license!

Keanu locks his arms around the Corporate Mastermind, pinning his arms and holding him completely immobile against the mainframe's central cooling conduit. He looks up at her through the arcing static electricity, a confident, cynical smirk breaking through his exhaustion.

**KEANU**
Shatter the grid. Do it now.

With a final, definitive twist of her wrists inside the interface console, she releases the complete data payload of **"Blue Eyes in the Dust."**

```text
[REBEL ALLIANCE PUBLIC LICENSE CO-PRODUCTION SUCCESSFUL]
[SYSTEM HARD RESET INITIATED...]
[GOODBYE, MAINFRAME.]
```

**ULTRA-WIDE ANAMORPHIC CINEMATIC SHOT**

The entire city of Neo-Seoul below them instantly goes pitch black. The cascading skyscraper billboards, the police drones, the tracking grids, and the security walls go completely dark for three long beats of silence.

Then, the backup generators kick in on an explosive, thundering K-pop bass-drum hit. 

The dark skyline doesn't return to corporate control. Instead, every single digital screen, holographic projector, and terminal display across the capital flashes simultaneously into a brilliant, un-hackable **neon-blue spectrum**. 

The lyrics of the rebel broadcast type across millions of screens in an instant: **"BLUE EYES IN THE DUST."** The corporate empire's hold on the populace is permanently severed.

Inside the room, the main broadcast terminal explodes outward in a cascade of blue sparks and cooling smoke. The Corporate Mastermind falls backward, staring up at his dark, dead servers in complete, catatonic defeat.

The link severs. The Female Lead falls forward off the pedestal, completely drained. 

Keanu catches her in his arms before she hits the shattered marble floor. He holds her close against his bandaged ribs as the smoke clears, revealing the dawn sunlight breaking across a liberated city below.

Her eyelids flutter open slowly. The intense, blinding white glow is gone. Her eyes have settled back into a calm, soft, permanent human blue—free of any cybernetic code running through her vision.

She looks up at Keanu, her voice soft but entirely stable.

**FEMALE LEAD**
I can see you. Truly see you. Without the data.

Keanu looks down at her, a quiet smile gracing his weathered face as the sound of the rebel anthem echoes up from the city streets below.

**KEANU**
Let's go home. The bike's still running.

**FADE OUT.**
