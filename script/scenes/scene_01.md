# 🎬 SCENE 01: THE BREACH

**EXT. UNDERGROUND VAULT - NEO-SEOUL - NIGHT**

A heavy, industrial bulkhead door sparks violently as a thermal torch cuts through its locking mechanism. Rain from the street level above leaks through the concrete ceiling, dripping onto a black leather motorcycle jacket.

**KEANU** (50s, weathered, eyes sharp behind tactical lenses) kicks the massive door open. Smoke rolls out. He steps inside, pulling a heavy-caliber revolver from his holster.

***

**INT. THE MAINFRAME VAULT - CONTINUOUS**

The room is clinical, white, and freezing. Rows of liquid-nitrogen server racks hum softly. In the center of the room sits a single, cylindrical glass stasis pod pulsating with red corporate data streams.

Keanu approaches the console, holstering his weapon. His gloved fingers fly across the holographic keyboard, overriding the security lock.

```text
[KEYBOARD OVERRIDE SUCCESSFUL]
[INITIATING REBEL ALLIANCE PUBLIC LICENSE DECRYPTION...]
```

The glass pod hisses, venting cold white steam across the floor. The security glass slides downward into the floor.

Inside stands the **FEMALE LEAD** (20s, blonde hair messy and damp). She wears a tattered black tank top and utility pants. Her hands are wrapped in stained, makeshift bandages. 

Her head is tilted down. She doesn't move. She looks like a lifeless corporate asset.

Keanu steps closer, reaching out a cautious, gloved hand toward her shoulder.

**KEANU**
Hey. Secure transport is outside. Wake up.

The moment his fingers touch her shoulder, her head snaps up with terrifying speed. 

She grabs his wrist, twisting it sharply. Keanu grunts, countering the movement instantly, pinning her arm behind her back. They freeze, locked in a tight, breathing-room standoff.

Slowly, her eyelids open. 

Her eyes do not look human. They are a brilliant, luminous **neon-blue**, laced with intricate, glowing cybernetic micro-circuitry. The digital pupils cycle rapidly, scanning Keanu's face like a barcode.

The blue light casts a sharp glow across the dirt smudges on her face and the scars on his jaw. 

**FEMALE LEAD**
*(Voice quiet, layered with a faint synthetic echo)*
You aren't the mainframe cleanup crew.

**KEANU**
*(A faint, cynical smirk cracks his lips)*
Not even close. You're the asset?

She looks past him toward the open door, where red security hazard lights begin to flash rhythmically down the hallway. The bass-heavy thrum of approaching corporate patrol drones vibrates through the floor tiles.

**FEMALE LEAD**
If we stay here, we're both deleted. 

**KEANU**
*(Releasing his grip, pulling his gun)*
I’ve got a bike on the surface. Can you ride?

She glances down at her bandaged hands, then locks her piercing blue eyes back onto his.

**FEMALE LEAD**
Fasten your helmet. I drive.

**FADE OUT.**
