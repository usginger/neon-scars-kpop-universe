# 🏍️ SCENE 02: THE SEOUL HIGHWAY CHASE

**EXT. VAULT EXIT - ROOFTOP ALLEYWAY - NIGHT**

A heavy steel door crashes open. KEANU and the FEMALE LEAD burst out into the driving Seoul rain. Torrential water drops instantly plaster her messy blonde hair to her forehead. 

Screaming sirens echo from the canyons between skyscrapers. Red and blue strobe lights paint the wet brick walls in a frantic rhythm.

In the center of the dark alley sits a brutalist, matte-black custom cruiser motorcycle, its engine warm and humming a low, mechanical bassline.

Keanu pulls a sleek, matte-black full-face helmet from the handlebars and tosses it to her. She catches it with her bandaged hands, sliding it effortlessly over her head. Her luminous neon-blue eyes shine intensely behind the tinted visor.

She kicks her leg over the frame, settling into the driver's seat. Keanu vaults onto the pillion seat behind her, drawing his heavy-caliber revolver.

**FEMALE LEAD**
Hold on.

She twists the throttle. The bike screeches, its rear tire chewing into the wet asphalt and throwing a massive rooster tail of water into the camera lens. They rocket out of the alleyway, plunging straight over the concrete barrier and onto the neon-slicked grid of the main highway.

***

**EXT. GANGNAM HIGHWAY - CONTINUOUS**

The traffic is a blur of high-speed automated transport pods. Overhead, massive digital billboards flash corporate propaganda, projecting towering holographic advertisements into the rainy sky.

```text
[ALERT: MAINFRAME BREACH IN PROGRESS]
[SYSTEM DETECTED REBEL ACTIVITY IN SECTOR 4]
[INITIATING TACTICAL INTERCEPT OVERRIDE]
```

Behind them, three sleek, predatory corporate interceptor drones—looking like matte-black hover-pods laced with crimson LED accents—drop down from the upper highway tiers. Their searchlights cut through the downpour, instantly locking onto the escaping motorcycle.

Keanu twists his torso around on the back of the accelerating bike, leveling his weapon.

*BANG. BANG.*

His heavy rounds puncture the front thruster of the leading drone. It sparks violently, twisting sideways and colliding with a concrete structural pillar in a blinding cloud of smoke and digital artifacts.

**KEANU**
(Shouting over the engine roar and the rain)
We’ve got company on our six! Two more coming down fast!

The remaining two drones accelerate, their automated tracking lasers projecting thin, bright red lines across Keanu's leather jacket, creeping up toward his neck.

The Female Lead looks into the rearview mirror. Her cybernetic blue eyes flare with computational data streams, calculating the trajectory of the automated traffic ahead.

**FEMALE LEAD**
(Through the helmet comms, calm and synthetic)
They control the lights. They're going to box us in at the bridge.

**KEANU**
Then don't stop.

***

**EXT. BANPO BRIDGE - CONTINUOUS**

Ahead, the massive suspension bridge spanning the Han River looms. Heavy steel security barricades begin to rise slowly from the asphalt, hydraulic fluid hissing, completely sealing off the exit route. Red hazard lights blink rapidly on the structural arches.

The Female Lead doesn't brake. She leans the bike lower into the curve, cutting directly between two civilian transport pods, accelerating straight toward the narrowing gap beneath the rising security barrier.

Behind them, the drones open fire. Pulsating energy rounds blast craters into the asphalt right at their heels, kicking up shards of concrete and boiling steam.

Keanu fires back, emptying his cylinder into the second drone's sensor eye. The drone veers wildly out of control, clipping the bridge railing and plummeting into the dark river below.

The final drone surges ahead, its searchlight blinding them from above. The structural barrier ahead is now three feet off the ground and rising fast—forming a crude, mechanical launch ramp.

The Female Lead locks her gaze onto the apex of the rising steel plate.

**FEMALE LEAD**
Hang on to something!

She rips the throttle to its absolute limit. The engine screams. 

The motorcycle hits the base of the rising barricade at 130 miles per hour. The suspension compresses completely, then explodes outward, launching the heavy bike high into the stormy air.

**SUPER SLOW-MOTION**

The world goes completely silent except for the heavy, driving trap-snare beat drop of the background track. 

The motorcycle flies airborne through the rainy night sky, framed perfectly against the sprawling, neon-lit skyline of downtown Seoul. Raindrops hang suspended around them like diamonds in the air. 

For a fraction of a second, the glowing neon-blue of her eyes behind the visor aligns perfectly with the distant corporate tower mainframe lights.

**FAST FORWARD BACK TO REAL TIME**

The bike slams hard back down onto the slick pavement on the opposite side of the barrier, bottoming out with a shower of sparks before stabilizing. 

Behind them, the final interceptor drone crashes violently directly into the fully raised steel wall, erupting into a brilliant ball of crimson fire.

The Female Lead stabilizes the bike's slide, leaning into a long exit ramp that plunges downward into a dark, unlit subterranean tunnel system. They vanish into the shadows, leaving the glowing city matrix behind them.

**FADE OUT.**
