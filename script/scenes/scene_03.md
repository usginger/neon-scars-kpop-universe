# 🏚️ SCENE 03: THE UNDERGROUND SAFEHOUSE

**INT. SUBTERRANEAN BUNKER - NEO-SEOUL UNDERGROUND - NIGHT**

A heavy steel door rattles shut, its manual deadbolts slamming home with a metallic, echoey thud. The engine of the custom cruiser motorcycle sputters, clicks, and dies, leaving the room in a cavernous, heavy silence.

The safehouse is a forgotten subway utility station. Exposed concrete walls are damp with condensation. Flickering fluorescent green and amber terminal monitors hum softly along the far wall, their long cables snaking across a floor littered with ancient electronic scrap.

The FEMALE LEAD kicks down the kickstand and painfully dismounts the bike. She rips her full-face helmet off, her blonde hair spilling down her shoulders. Her face is pale, slick with cold sweat and rainwater. She collapses onto a metal workbench, breathing heavily.

KEANU stands by the door, his eyes scanning a security monitor showing the rain-washed alley above. Satisfied they weren't followed, he unzips his soaked leather jacket and peels it off. Underneath, a dark gray tactical shirt is heavily saturated with blood on the left side of his ribs.

He walks over to a rusted medical supply trunk, pops the latches, and pulls out an old cybernetic diagnostic kit and synthetic bandage wraps. He tosses a clean towel to her.

**KEANU**
Clean the rain off your neck implants. If the water shorts the internal telemetry, you'll go blind before sunrise.

The Female Lead catches the towel with her bandaged hands, trembling slightly. She wipes down her face, then gently dabs at the glowing, exposed ports at the base of her skull. The neon-blue light of her eyes flickers erratically, dimming from vibrant cyan to a pale, washed-out sky blue.

**FEMALE LEAD**
(Voice shaking, digital echo glitching)
The... the bridge jump. The impact cracked the primary optical heat sink. My internal matrix temperature is spiking at ninety-eight percent capacity. 

Keanu steps closer, setting down the diagnostic scanner. He leans in to examine the glowing synthetic circuitry running up her neck. He works with clinical, steady hands—the hands of a man who has patched up a hundred broken operatives.

**KEANU**
You forced the processor to recalculate the traffic grid variables at a hundred and thirty miles per hour. You're lucky your neural core didn't fry on the asphalt. Turn around.

She hesitates, looking up into his weathered face, checking for any sign of betrayal. Seeing none, she rotates her shoulders away from him.

Keanu pulls out a small, metallic cooling syringe from the medical kit. He clicks it into the port at the base of her neck. A soft hiss echoes through the bunker as a glowing blue cryo-fluid injects into her system.

Immediately, her posture relaxes. The rapid glitching in her voice settles into a smooth, rhythmic hum. The deep neon-blue intensity of her eyes returns, casting long sapphire shadows across the dark concrete wall.

**FEMALE LEAD**
(Quietly, looking at the terminal screens)
Internal temperature stabilizing. Thank you, Keanu.

**KEANU**
Save it. We aren't out of the grid yet.

He turns around, gripping the edge of the workbench. A sharp groan escapes his teeth as he finally addresses his own wound. He pulls his shirt up, revealing a deep, smoking graze wound along his ribcage—the skin singed black and bleeding from a corporate drone's near-miss laser discharge.

The Female Lead slides off the workbench. Her boots click softly on the concrete as she approaches him. She takes the synthetic bandage wrap out of his hand.

**FEMALE LEAD**
You're bleeding. Sit.

Keanu looks at her for a brief second, his usual cold, professional mask slipping just enough to show the exhausting toll of a lifetime on the run. He sits heavily on a crate.

She kneels beside him, her bandaged fingers surprisingly gentle as she applies a burning, quick-seal antiseptic spray to his side, causing him to hiss through his teeth and tighten his jaw. She begins tightly wrapping the clean white bandages around his torso.

**FEMALE LEAD**
(Looking down at her work)
The corporate mastermind... he won't stop with three interceptor units. He knows exactly what I took from the mainframe vault. 

**KEANU**
He knows you took the Master Cipher Key. What he doesn't know is why a corporate experiment decided to hand it over to the Underground Rebel Broadcast. Why run? You had security. You had a future inside the tower.

She finishes tying off the bandage around his ribs. She steps back, standing under the dim, flickering amber light of the safehouse monitors. She turns her face directly to him, her glowing blue eyes blazing with a fierce, unmistakable human defiance.

**FEMALE LEAD**
They didn't build these eyes to let me see the world, Keanu. They built them to analyze it, format it, and cage it. I didn't defect to save the data. I defected to save the piece of myself they couldn't rewrite into code.

Keanu slowly stands up, testing the tightness of the bandage around his ribs. He walks over to her side, looking at the blinking green diagnostic streams of data running down the monitor screens. For the first time, a genuine, soft look replaces his cynical stare.

**KEANU**
Then let's make sure they never get the chance to overwrite you. Get some rest. At dawn, we upload the cipher and shatter their grid for good.

She gives him a subtle, faint half-smile, leaning back against the mainframe console as the heavy hum of the safehouse equipment keeps the oppressive darkness of the Seoul underground at bay.

**FADE OUT.**
