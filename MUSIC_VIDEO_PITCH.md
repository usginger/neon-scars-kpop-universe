# 🎬 Music Video Concept & Lighting Storyboard

### 💡 Visual Contrasts
* **The Rebel Space:** Warm golden blonde highlights, organic messiness, and sweaty street elements.
* **The Corporate Space:** Hyper-sharp, symmetrical, clinical cold neon blue, surgical white, and warning red.

### 📌 The Key Sequence (The Bridge Drop)
1. **The Snap:** The instant the CEO's verse begins, all warm gold tones instantly drain from the frame.
2. **The Freeze:** The street rebels glitch out under a massive digital blue and red tracking grid projected from the high-rise penthouse.
3. **The Shatter:** During the final chorus, the warm gold energy of the heroes overloads the corporate grid, shattering the glass windows of the skyscraper.
