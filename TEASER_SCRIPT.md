# 🎬 Companion Anime Teaser Scene: "The Conglomerate Breach"

* **Setting:** The rain-slicked glass balcony of the Nexus Tower Penthouse. 
* **Atmosphere:** Clinical neon blue lighting, heavy rain drumming against glass, low ambient synthesizer drone humming in the background.

---

### [SCENE START]

**(The camera slowly sweeps across a hyper-minimalist corporate office. The DEVIL CEO stands looking out the floor-to-ceiling glass wall. He adjusts his immaculate tech-suit cuffs. The reflection of the chaotic, neon-pink city streets flickers across his face.)**

**DEVIL CEO**  
*(Calm, measured, dripping with ice)*  
You both run well. I’ll grant you that. But a mouse inside a circuit board is still just a mouse inside a cage.

**(A loud crash echoes from the elevator bay behind him. The heavy metal doors drop with a thud, heavily dented. The BATTLE REBEL steps through the smoke, his breathing heavy, hand resting tightly on his combat blade. His golden hair is damp from the storm.)**

**BATTLE REBEL**  
*(Grit in his teeth, voice low and dangerous)*  
The cage is already broken, old man. We didn't come to negotiate. We came to delete your system.

**DEVIL CEO**  
*(Turning around slowly, a faint mocking smile appearing)*  
Bold words for a street rat running on borrowed time. Look at yourself. Scars on your skin, dirt in your lungs... Do you truly believe a nomad belongs in the gutter with you? 

**(From the shattered elevator shaft, the GOLDEN NOMAD steps into the light, her black leather jacket gleaming under the flashing warning alarms. Her ice-blue eyes lock directly onto the CEO.)**

**GOLDEN NOMAD**  
*(Steady, unyielding tone)*  
I don't belong to the gutter. And I certainly don't belong in your database. 

**DEVIL CEO**  
*(His smile fades; his voice drops an octave)*  
You are the crown jewel of this city's design. I built this paradise around your parameters. If I cannot have your loyalty... I will rewrite your code from scratch.

**(The CEO raises his hand, and a sharp, clinical blue digital tracker grid projects across the entire floor, locking onto their positions. The heavy industrial trap-step bass from the song begins to swell.)**

**BATTLE REBEL**  
*(Glancing at the Nomad, a smirk cracking through his exhaustion)*  
Hey, Nomad. Looks like he still thinks he's running the show. Ready to show him how a real glitch works?

**GOLDEN NOMAD**  
*(Reaching down to rev the digital ignition on her wrist gauntlet)*  
Let's shatter the grid.

**(The beat drops violently. The screen cuts to black as the title card "NEON SCARS" flashes in high-contrast amber-gold.)**

### [SCENE END]
